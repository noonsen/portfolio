Feeding time at the koi pond, shot straight down after dark.

The water barely reads as water — mostly it's a black mirror holding the
sky's reflection, broken up wherever a fish shoulders through the surface.
First frame: the mixed crowd, ogon and kohaku shoving in among the plainer
brown carp. Second frame: almost all red-and-white, a solid raft of them
turning the surface over.
