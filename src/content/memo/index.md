Time flows through us, like wind through a corridor.
<br />There are always moments that carry a particular scent or glow, lingering briefly before they fade.
<br />Some feelings can't be held by a camera, some memories are hard to capture in a photograph.
<br />Maybe keeping a written backup of a fleeting feeling is the gentlest, yet most effective, way to resist forgetting.

## 2025 Notes

### New Home

The night we moved in, we set a small lamp on the floor, boxes still piled around the room. We used a cardboard box as a table and ordered takeout. It wasn't until we woke up the next day that it finally felt like we'd really moved in.

### A Small-Town Trip

Decided on a whim to visit a seaside town, arrived early in the morning, and had hot soy milk in front of the station first. Walked along the seawall — the sea breeze kept blowing my hat askew. Tucked the train ticket into my wallet on the way back.

### Night Return

Came back that evening to the intersection I always pass — the shop sign had changed twice. The rain had just stopped, the road glistening. Stood there a while before walking on.

### Secondhand Bookstore

Ducked into a secondhand bookstore when it rained, the little bell by the door ringing the whole time. Picked out a used essay collection and read a few pages inside. The owner wrapped the book in old newspaper.

### Candlelit Dinner

A power outage once, so we ate by candlelight. The food was simple, but the room was quiet enough to hear the rain. Restocked the spare candles afterward.

### Framed Photos Together

While sorting through photo albums, picked two old photos to put in the same frame. Placed it by the window, where the morning light is just right. I glance at it a little longer whenever I pass by.

### Reunion After Years

A friend I hadn't seen in years came to visit, and we walked together to the river. Talked late into the night, still plenty left unsaid. Promised before parting not to let so much time pass again.

### Night Runs

Started running at night once it turned cool, always along the same long street. Streetlight after streetlight passed, footsteps clear in the quiet. Slept soundly after getting home.

> Nothing major happened this year, but it left behind quite a few moments worth remembering.

## 2024 Notes

### An Evening by the River

Went to sit by the river one evening, someone playing music in the distance. Light breeze, scattered glints of light on the water. Stayed until dark before heading back.

### A Handwritten Letter

Wrote a letter to a friend far away, used up two sheets of paper. Only near the end did I realize how much I don't usually say. Read it over once more before mailing it.

### A Small-Town Getaway

Went to a nearby small town over the weekend and had a local breakfast. Brought back a few postcards, now pinned up by my desk. The trip was short, but comfortable.

## 2023 Notes

### First Snow

The first snowfall was light, the streets very quiet. Stood by the window watching it for a long time.


### A Corner of Books

Repainted the old bookshelf and set out the books I read often, leaving one shelf deliberately empty. Turned on a small lamp at night and sat there a while.

### Old Friends Reunited

Old friends gathered for a meal. Everyone had changed a little, but the laughter was still familiar. My heart felt warm when it was over.
